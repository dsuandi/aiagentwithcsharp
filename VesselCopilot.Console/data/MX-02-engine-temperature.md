Title: MX-02 Main engine temperature alarm response
Type: Procedure
Revision: 2

SYNTHETIC - workshop use only.

Trigger. Jacket-water outlet temperature above 90 C, or a swing of more than 8 C
within one hour.

Immediate actions. Reduce engine load, check the jacket-water pump and the
thermostatic valve, and inform the chief engineer.

Follow-up. Record readings every 15 minutes until stable and raise a maintenance
draft for inspection at the next port.
