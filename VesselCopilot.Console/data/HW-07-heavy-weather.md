Title: HW-07 Heavy-weather passage procedure
Type: Procedure
Revision: 4

SYNTHETIC - workshop use only.

Scope. Applies when wind reaches Beaufort force 7 or swell exceeds 4 metres.

Before heavy weather the master reduces speed to limit slamming and heavy rolling,
confirms all deck cargo lashings and briefs the crew on restricted deck access.

During heavy weather the officer of the watch logs position, wind, swell and speed
every hour. A loose lashing is reported at once and re-secured only when safe.

After heavy weather the chief officer inspects lashings and hatch covers and records
the findings in the deck log within 12 hours. Revision 4 replaces revision 3.
